Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Atreemology ("the app," "we," "our") is a vocabulary-learning app. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app handles and what it doesn't. The short version: there's no account system, nothing you do inside the app is sent to a server, and we don't run analytics or advertising of any kind.

What we don't collect

  • No account or sign-up is required to use Atreemology, so we don't collect names, email addresses, or passwords.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs of any kind inside the app.
  • We don't sell, rent, or share any data, because we don't collect any to begin with.

What's stored, and where

Everything Atreemology tracks about your learning (planted roots, word mastery, streaks, quiz history, and your field journal) is saved using your browser's local storage, directly on your own device. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or anyone else. If you clear your browser's site data, uninstall the app, or switch devices, that progress does not carry over automatically.

You can export a copy of your progress at any time from the Profile screen ("Export my progress"), and you can permanently erase it from your device using "Reset all progress" on the same screen, or by clearing your browser's site data for atreemology.com.

The website itself

This marketing website (the pages other than the app itself) loads one third-party resource: Google Fonts, used to display the "Baloo 2" typeface in headlines. Loading a font this way means your browser makes a request to Google's servers, which may log standard technical information such as your IP address, the same as any web request. We don't control what Google does with that request; see Google's privacy policy for details. The website does not otherwise use cookies or tracking scripts.

Children's privacy

Atreemology is a general-audience vocabulary app and is not directed at children under 13. Since the app collects no personal information from anyone, regardless of age, there is no personal data from children for us to have, store, or need to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds optional accounts or sync), we'll update this page and revise the "last updated" date above before that change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
