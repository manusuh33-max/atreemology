Frequently asked questions

Is Atreemology free?

Yes. The full Foundational Grove (26 hand-verified root families and every word that grows from them) is free, with no account, subscription, or ads. More root families are on the roadmap for future updates.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Atreemology doesn't have accounts at all right now: you can open it and start planting roots immediately. Your progress is saved automatically on your own device.

Does my progress sync across devices?

Not yet. Since there's no account system, your forest is saved locally in your browser on whichever device you use it on. If you want to move it, use the "Export my progress" button on the Profile screen to save a backup file, and clearing your browser data will remove your saved forest, so it's worth exporting a backup now and then.

Does it work offline?

Once a page has loaded in your browser, most of the app works without a connection, since everything runs locally and progress is saved to your device rather than a server.

How is this different from a flashcard app?

Flashcard apps usually hand you disconnected words to memorize one at a time. Atreemology teaches the root those words grow from first, so learning one root, like "dict" (to speak), unlocks a whole family of words (predict, dictate, verdict) at once, instead of treating each one as a separate fact to memorize.

What's spaced repetition, and do I have to manage it myself?

No, it happens automatically. After you learn a word, you rate how well you knew it (again, hard, good, or easy), and Atreemology schedules it to come back for review right before you'd naturally start forgetting it, rather than at a fixed interval.

Is there an Android or iOS app?

Atreemology works as a web app in any modern mobile or desktop browser, and can be added to your phone's home screen like a native app. A Google Play listing is in progress; there's currently no plan for an Apple App Store release.

Who verifies the word roots?

Every root and word family that's live in the app has been individually checked against its actual etymology, not auto-generated or bulk-imported. Roots that haven't been verified yet are shown on the roadmap as locked, not shipped as plantable content.

Can I get my data or delete it?

Your progress lives only in your browser's local storage, so you're always in control of it: export it from the Profile screen at any time, or clear it by using "Reset all progress" on the same screen (or clearing your browser's site data). See the privacy policy for details.
